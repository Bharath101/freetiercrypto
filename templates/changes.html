Free tier changes

A dated record of changes to the free tiers and cheapest paid plans of the tools we track.

How this log works

  • Every day a scheduled job (scripts/check_pricing.py) re-reads each tool's pricing page, pulls out the lines that mention prices, plans and limits, and compares them with the last saved snapshot.
  • When something differs, the job writes it to a diff file for review. It does not edit this page by itself, because pricing pages also change for harmless reasons (sale banners, layout, currency by visitor location).
  • A person opens the pricing page, confirms the change, updates the dataset, and adds a dated entry here with the before, the after and the source link.
